Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version
  • LogShift checks you in and out of your workplace automatically. Your location is used on your phone only to tell whether you're inside the workplace area. We never send your location, a map or your route to anyone.
  • Your workplace sees times and hours: when you arrived, left and took breaks, plus any fix or leave requests you send.
  • We store your name and email from Google sign-in so your workplace knows the hours are yours.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains what information LogShift ("we", "us", the "app") handles, why, who can see it, and your choices. It applies to the LogShift mobile app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

LogShift uses Google sign-in. When you sign in we receive and store:

  • your email address,
  • your name, and
  • a Google account identifier used to authenticate you.

We do not receive your Google password.

Workplace membership

When a workplace adds your email, or you join with a 6-digit code, we store that you belong to that workplace and your role there (employee or owner).

Attendance

We store the times you are checked in and out, whether each time was automatic or entered by you, and any reason you add (for example when you check out early). From these the app works out your hours and breaks.

Fix and leave requests

If you ask to correct a time or ask for leave, we store the request, the reason you give, and whether your workplace approved or declined it.

Workplace settings (owners)

If you run a workplace, we store its name and area, its shift (work days, start and end times, when someone counts as late), and its check-in spot: one set of coordinates for the gate and a check-in distance. This is the location of the workplace, not of any person.

Your location - used on your phone, never collected

LogShift asks for location access, including "Allow all the time", so it can check you in and out even when the app is closed. Your phone compares your position with the workplace's check-in spot and only tells the app when you cross that boundary. Your location is not sent to us or to your workplace, and we do not keep a history of where you have been. When tracking is paused nothing is logged at all.

What we do not collect

  • Your location, route or the places you visit.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data.
  • Third-party analytics or behavioral data.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • sign you in and keep your account secure,
  • show you your own hours, breaks and history,
  • show your workplace your times, hours and requests so they can run attendance, and
  • send you notifications you have turned on (check-in and check-out alerts, a daily summary). These are created on your phone.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the app.

3. Who can see your information

  • You see your own attendance and requests.
  • Owners of your workplace see the name, email, attendance times, hours and requests of the people in that workplace. Other employees do not see your attendance.
  • LogShift administrators (the people who set up workplaces on LogShift) can see workplaces and the people in them to provide support.

4. How your information is stored

Your attendance is first saved on your phone, so the app works without a connection, and is then uploaded to Supabase, our backend and authentication provider. Our database is hosted in Mumbai, India. Access is limited by database rules so each person only sees what is described in section 3.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In - to sign you in with your Google account. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase - to store your account and attendance. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play services - your phone's location services decide when you cross the workplace boundary. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

6. Data retention and deleting your account

We keep your information while you have a LogShift account. If a workplace is deleted, its members, attendance and requests are deleted with it.

  • Data on your phone: sign out, uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device settings.
  • Your account and everything stored on our servers: email tahalakdawala03@gmail.com from the Google account you use with LogShift, with the subject "Delete my LogShift account". We delete your account, workplace memberships, attendance and requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your workplace may need attendance records for payroll or legal reasons; if so, ask them for a copy before you delete your account.

7. Children's privacy

LogShift is a workplace attendance app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, CCPA, or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated within the app where appropriate.
